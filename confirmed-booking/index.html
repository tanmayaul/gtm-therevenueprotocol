Questions advisors ask

Answered the way we answer them on the call.

What counts as a held appointment?

The prospect attended at the booked time, you were on the call, and they met the profile you set at onboarding. That is the only appointment you pay for.

What happens if they don't show up?

Nothing on your bill. The no-show goes back to our caller desk and is replaced with another qualified prospect.

How do you verify assets?

The quiz is a starting point, not the source of truth. A US-based caller phones every lead within minutes of the form and confirms age, investable assets, and whether the money can move. Most raw leads stop there.

Where do the prospects come from?

Meta and Google ads under our consumer retirement brand, aimed at people planning their retirement. They take a two-minute quiz with a $250K floor and a verified phone number. Then a phone screen. Then a short video and a written primer before the meeting.

Do they know who I am before the call?

Yes. Our caller books the meeting during the screen, introduces you by name, and explains why you are the right fit. Reminders run to the day of the meeting.

Can I choose which states and which tiers?

Yes. At onboarding you set the states you are licensed in, the asset floor, your booking calendar, and any disclosure language. Nothing outside that lands on your calendar.

What if the prospect is in the wrong tier, or not qualified?

A wrong tier rebills at the right tier, in either direction. If a prospect was not qualified, flag it within 48 hours with the reason and we credit it after checking the intake recording.

What does an appointment cost?

Pricing is per held appointment and set by the prospect's verified assets, in four tiers from $250K to $10M+. We go through the numbers with you on the call.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Cancel by email, no phone call required.

Why not seminars or a lead vendor?

Seminars fill the room with people who came for the meal, and they cost the same to feed as the buyers. Lead vendors charge when the form is submitted, so fake names and no-shows land on your card. We bill on the held appointment, so everything upstream of it is our cost, not yours.

04Value Proposition

The Regency Leads value proposition

So you pay for a held appointment, and everything upstream of it is our cost.

The guarantee
$0

If they don't show, you don't pay

A no-show or a cancel is replaced with another qualified prospect and never billed. Held means attended, with you on the call, above the floor you set.

The screen
$250K+

Verified movable assets on every appointment

A US-based caller confirms age, investable assets, and whether the money can move before anything is booked. The quiz is a starting point, not the source of truth.

The speed
Minutes

From form to our first call

Every lead is called while they are still at the screen, so the person on your calendar is the one who filled out the form.

The filter
70 to 80%

Of raw leads never reach you

Most of what digital ads produce is screened out on the phone. What gets through has been asked the same questions you would ask.

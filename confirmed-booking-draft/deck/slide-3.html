Challenges in retirement lead gen

That rule exists because of what advisors tell us about the leads they buy.

Seminars fill the room with the wrong people

Most retirement leads come from seminars and generic classes. The room is full of people who came for the meal, and they cost the same to feed as the buyers.

Lead vendors bill for the lead, not the meeting

Online lead vendors charge when the form is submitted. Fake names, fake phone numbers, and no-shows all land on your card, and the vendor is paid either way.

Every conversation starts from zero

Nobody has explained who you are or what a plan with you looks like, so the first fifteen minutes go to credibility instead of their situation.

Credibility is rebuilt on every call

Because the leads arrive cold, advisors host expensive events just to establish credibility. Skip the event and the first call carries that whole job.

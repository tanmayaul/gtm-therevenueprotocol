05The Appointment Engine

The appointment engine

Three things happen upstream before a prospect reaches your calendar.

Find the retiree

  • •Ads under our consumer retirement brand, aimed at people planning their retirement
  • •A two-minute quiz with an asset floor, phone verification, and consent
  • •Assets and timeline verified again on the phone

Teach them first

  • •A short video and a written primer before the call
  • •By the meeting they know what a retirement plan review is for
  • •The first meeting can go straight to their situation

Vouch for you

  • •Our caller introduces you by name and explains why you are the right fit
  • •They hear your background and why they are being booked with you specifically
  • •By the meeting they know who you are

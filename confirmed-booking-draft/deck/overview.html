03Challenges

Challenges in retirement lead gen

That rule exists because of what advisors tell us about the leads they buy.

Seminars fill the room with the wrong people

Most retirement leads come from seminars and generic classes. The room is full of people who came for the meal, and they cost the same to feed as the buyers.

Lead vendors bill for the lead, not the meeting

Online lead vendors charge when the form is submitted. Fake names, fake phone numbers, and no-shows all land on your card, and the vendor is paid either way.

Every conversation starts from zero

Nobody has explained who you are or what a plan with you looks like, so the first fifteen minutes go to credibility instead of their situation.

Credibility is rebuilt on every call

Because the leads arrive cold, advisors host expensive events just to establish credibility. Skip the event and the first call carries that whole job.

04Value Proposition

The Regency Leads value proposition

So you pay for a held appointment, and everything upstream of it is our cost.

The guarantee
$0

If they don't show, you don't pay

A no-show or a cancel is replaced with another qualified prospect and never billed. Held means attended, with you on the call, above the floor you set.

The screen
$250K+

Verified movable assets on every appointment

A US-based caller confirms age, investable assets, and whether the money can move before anything is booked. The quiz is a starting point, not the source of truth.

The speed
Minutes

From form to our first call

Every lead is called while they are still at the screen, so the person on your calendar is the one who filled out the form.

The filter
70 to 80%

Of raw leads never reach you

Most of what digital ads produce is screened out on the phone. What gets through has been asked the same questions you would ask.
